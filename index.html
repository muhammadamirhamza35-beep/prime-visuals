Why we work in 3-month plans.

A strong page is not built in a week. We need time to learn your brand, and your customers need time to start recognising it. One month is too short for both, so we start at three.

Why not just 1 month?

In one month we would spend most of the time learning your brand, and you would be left with a handful of posts and no steady look. Three months gives us time to learn, improve and show results, and keeps your page active long enough for people to notice.

  1. Month 1We learn your brand

    We study your products, customers and competitors, then find the colors, style and tone that suit you. Your first posters and videos set the look.

  2. Month 2We sharpen it

    We see which posts people like and which they skip, and use that to improve every design and video that follows.

  3. Month 3Your brand looks consistent

    Your page now has a steady rhythm of 45 posters and 12 videos. Customers start to recognise you at a glance.
